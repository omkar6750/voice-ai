## Clear technical writing

Apply Clear Technical English (`ste`) by default: explain what a component does
and why it matters; use concrete subjects, active verbs and one main idea per
sentence. Define unfamiliar terms, put steps in execution order, and distinguish
confirmed behavior, assumptions and recommendations. State uncertainty and how to
verify it. Keep explanations accurate when shortening them.
